02 /

About

Who / what / why

The whole product,
not just the code.

I was a founding engineer at RVshare. Since then I have split my time between small product shops and my own builds, writing the code, shaping the business, and staying close to hardware and aviation.

My edge is working across the whole stack a company has: the storefront, the firmware, the ops inbox, and the spreadsheet that keeps it all together. That range matters when the product spans checkout, telemetry, fulfillment, and field testing.

Earlier · Movable

My first job was building wrist-worn activity monitors, with a sync client and cloud tools for reviewing movement and competition. Wearable hardware before the Apple Watch existed.
